**Brainrot Bandits** is a Steal-a-Brainrot style tycoon game on Roblox. Absurd brainrot
characters — Cappuccino Golem, Banana Shark, Tung Tung Sahur, Ballerina Cappuccina and
more — spawn on a central conveyor lane and walk it. Claim one, carry it overhead,
dash it home to your base, and it starts earning you cash every second. Other players
can slap you to make you drop it, so the walk home is never safe.

## How to play

1. Wait by the lane for a brainrot you like — rarer ones pay more per second.
2. Touch it (or use its prompt) to claim it. It costs its price in Cash.
3. Carry it overhead back to your own base and step on the delivery zone.
   It moves to a pad and starts earning $/sec, forever.
4. Slap carriers to make them drop their brainrot, then grab it yourself.
5. Spend Cash in the shop (speed shoes, steel glove, lucky charm), hit the
   rebirth shrine to reset Cash for a permanent income multiplier, and watch
   for events: Rainbow hour and Mythic Hunt recolor the whole world and boost
   the spawns.

## Features

- **Conveyor lane spawner** — characters spawn every few seconds with rarity
  weights (Common 55 / Rare 25 / Epic 12 / Legendary 6 / Mythic 2) and Gold
  (x5), Diamond (x12) and Rainbow (x10) mutations.
- **Claim, carry, deliver, steal** — overhead two-hand carry pose, slap-to-drop
  with camera shake, free re-claim of dropped characters.
- **6 player bases** — each with 6 display pads, an owner sign, and a delivery zone.
- **Economy + rebirth** — per-second income ticks, rebirth ladder
  (10K / 50K / 250K / 1M / 5M / 25M) with multiplier 1 + rebirths.
- **Shop** — speed shoes, steel glove, lucky charm, bought with Cash.
- **Live events** — Rainbow and Mythic Hunt events change the lighting, plus a
  boss fight and lucky-block stands.
- **Richest-player board** and an admin panel for the experience owner.
- **Juicy client** — warm afternoon lighting with bloom, spawn portals,
  sparkles, income popups, Mythic aura rings, emotes, sound effects, and a
  loading screen.
